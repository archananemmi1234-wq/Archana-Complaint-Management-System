How It Works

A simple step-by-step process to submit complaints, connect with departments and track complaint progress.

Simple Process. Better Management.

ComplaintCare makes the complaint process easier for users and administrators. Users can register, submit complaints and monitor updates from one place.


Administrators can review submitted complaints, assign departments and update their progress. This helps maintain an organized complaint system.

Three Simple Steps

Follow these steps to use our Online Complaint Management System.

1

Register & Login

Create your account by entering your basic details. Login securely to access the complaint management dashboard.

2

Submit Your Complaint

Select the relevant department, enter a complaint subject and describe your issue. Submit the details through the online form.

3

Track & Get Updates

View your complaint history and check its current status. Administrators can review and update the complaint.

Understand Complaint Status

Every complaint moves through a status based on its progress.

🕒

Pending

The complaint has been submitted and is waiting for review.

⚙️

In Progress

The complaint is being reviewed or handled by the assigned department.

✅

Resolved

The complaint has been addressed and marked as resolved.
