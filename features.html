Powerful Features

ComplaintCare provides a simple and organized platform to submit complaints, monitor progress and manage complaint records efficiently.

What Our System Offers

Useful tools that make complaint management easier.

📝

Easy Complaint Submission

Students can submit complaints online by entering a subject, category, department and description.

🔍

Complaint Tracking

Users can view their submitted complaints and check the latest status from their dashboard.

🔐

Secure Login

Separate user and administrator login with password protection and session management.

🏢

Department Management

Complaints can be directed to the appropriate college department for better organization.

⚙️

Admin Control

Administrators can view complaint records and update complaint status when required.

📊

Dashboard Overview

Summary cards help administrators understand the number of users, departments and complaints.

Organized Complaint Management

ComplaintCare reduces manual complaint handling by storing complaint details in a centralized database.

Users can submit complaints through the website, while administrators can review records and manage their progress.

  • Simple and user-friendly interface
  • Centralized complaint records
  • Department-wise complaint organization
  • Separate user and admin access
  • Quick complaint status updates
